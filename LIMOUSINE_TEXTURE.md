# Limousinenfolierung – Version 12

Generierungsmodus: Referenzgestützte Neugenerierung einer flachen Textur mit der eingebauten Bildgenerierung. Die Fahrzeugfolierung im Originalbild diente als Stilvorlage. Ein einziges Bild wurde erstellt; keine Varianten und keine Hochskalierung.

Gespeicherte Projektdatei: `/workspace/sites/lumina-leaf-3d/dist/assets/limousine-side-wrap-v12.png`

Native Auflösung: 2172 × 724 Pixel. Dieselbe Textur wird auf beiden Karosserieseiten verwendet. Die UV-Ausrichtung berücksichtigt die entgegengesetzten Blickrichtungen, damit die Schrift beidseitig lesbar bleibt. Motorhaube, Dach und Kofferraum verwenden schwarzen Lack ohne Bildtextur.

Primary request: create exactly ONE panoramic image approximately 3:1 width to height, at the maximum native image detail available. Entire canvas is flat printable artwork on solid rich black. Crisp dense stylized botanical foliage, predominantly vibrant emerald and lime green leaves, with restrained red, purple, gold and white accent leaves. Small restrained warm light accents may be round glints only, never star-shaped. Closely match the reference limousine’s colorful angular leaves and elegant green hotel lettering.
Composition: leaves flow across the whole wide rectangle and crop naturally over both left and right edges. Keep an open black area behind the central hotel name. Name centered at approximately 50% image height, occupying the central 34% of image width. Below it, at approximately 72% image height, center a single horizontal row of EXACTLY FIVE evenly spaced gold five-point stars. Keep foliage flowing around this central name-and-stars group.
Text (verbatim): "Lumina Leaf Hotel". Render all three words correctly and legibly in elegant flowing emerald-green cursive script, inspired closely by the existing limousine lettering. Spell L-u-m-i-n-a L-e-a-f H-o-t-e-l. One line of text only.
Constraints: EXACTLY FIVE gold five-point stars total, all directly underneath the name, no other stars anywhere. Black background clearly visible behind the name. Clean sharp high-detail print design, edge-to-edge rectangle, no border, no watermark, no extra wording.
Avoid: vehicles, limousine silhouettes, wheels, doors, windows, perspective, mockups, garages, hotel buildings, landscapes, people, scenery, physical shadows, physical lighting, reflections, gradients suggesting metal or glass, any rendered surface. This is flat 2D artwork only.
